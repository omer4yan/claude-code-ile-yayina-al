About me

Hi, I'm Ömer.

Senior IT Technical Support Specialist

Since 2021 I have been working in the IT team of Konveyör Beyaz Eşya ve Otomotiv Yan. San. Tic. A.Ş. I started as an assistant technical support specialist, became a technical support specialist in September 2024 and a senior technical support specialist in April 2026. My work spans a wide area, from user support to network and field infrastructure, and from cloud migrations to systems on the production floor.

Along the way I have worked on projects such as the Microsoft 365 cloud migration, field infrastructure support during the SAP S/4HANA migration, the Enterprise Wi-Fi and SD-WAN transformation, and the rollout of the MES infrastructure.

I studied Computer Programming at Istanbul University (2010–2012) and at the Faculty of Economics of Anadolu University (2013–2016).

Recently I have become curious about artificial intelligence. I don't just want to follow the field; I want to learn and build things. The 'Claude Code ile Yayına Al' course was a step I took out of that curiosity. I'm continuing to apply what I've learned and to improve myself.
